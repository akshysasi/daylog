Daylog 📖

A personal Android app designed to help you reflect on your days, build better routines, and stay consistent with the things that matter to you.

Daylog combines daily journaling and routine tracking in one simple, private space.

🎯 Goal

The idea behind Daylog is simple:

Reflect on your day. Keep your routines in check. Become more consistent.

Instead of using separate apps for journaling, habits, and daily planning, Daylog aims to bring these parts of everyday life together in one focused application.

✨ Planned Features

The project will be developed gradually, with features being added one at a time.

📝 Daily journaling

✅ Daily routine tracking

📅 Journal and routine history

🔥 Streak tracking

📊 Progress and statistics

🔔 Routine and journaling reminders

😊 Mood tracking

🎯 Personal goals

🌙 Dark mode

🔒 Privacy-focused local storage

☁️ Backup and synchronization
